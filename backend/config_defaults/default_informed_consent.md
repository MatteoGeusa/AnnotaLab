# Informed Consent

## Welcome to the Study

Thank you for participating in this research study. Please read the following information carefully before proceeding.

## Your Task

You will be asked to read and classify a series of short texts. The estimated time to complete all tasks is approximately **15–20 minutes**. The goal of this study is to improve automated systems for analysing online discourse.

## Your Data and Privacy

This task is **anonymous**. We do not collect any personally identifiable information. We will only save:

- Your responses and annotations
- Your Prolific ID, which is required exclusively to confirm your completion and authorise your payment on the platform

Your data will be used solely for academic research purposes and will never be shared with third parties.

## Your Rights

Participation is **voluntary**. You may stop at any time. If you decide not to finish, simply close this page and click **"Return submission"** on Prolific. In this case, your partial data will not be saved or used, but we will not be able to process your payment.

## Agreement

By clicking the button below, you confirm that:

- You are at least **18 years old**
- You have read and understood this information
- You **consent** to participate in this study under the described conditions
